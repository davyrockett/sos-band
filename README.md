# SOS Band

The Sounds of Service (SOS) Band's setlist, chord charts, stage plot, gear list and mixer routing.
Live: https://davyrockett.github.io/sos-band/

- **Setlist**: tap a song to open its chart and print it. **Print all charts** makes one PDF of the whole set, in order.
  **Print list** prints just the song titles.
- Tap the gig name at the top to switch between gigs.
- **Stage plot**, **Gear**, **Mixer** and **Band**: what to bring and where everyone goes. Each has a Print button.
- Add it to your phone's home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) and the charts you've opened work with no signal.

This app is view-only. David updates it from his editor (davyrockett/sos-band-editor); its files are copied in from there.
